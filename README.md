One funny line for the [One Line, One Laugh](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deysumbrella-one-line-one-laugh-e0cdcda9) bounty on make.fun — a single joke, capped at 140 characters, judged pairwise by a deadpan comedy-club judge.

## The line

> My Roomba mapped my flat in 12 minutes, then spent an hour stuck under the couch judging my life choices.

105 characters — 75% of the 140-character cap. First-person voice, one concrete detail (12 minutes vs an hour stuck under the couch), judgement twist at the end: the robot that mapped the flat in minutes is the one passing judgement.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. Run it with:

```sh
node main.ts
```
